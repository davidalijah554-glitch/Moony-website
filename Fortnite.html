FORTNITE PRO TIPS

Welcome to Fortnite PRO Tips by MOONY™. Fortnite is one of the best games in the world and is played by millions of people. If you want to compete at the highest level, use these tips to help improve your gameplay. However, remember that only consistent practice can truly help you become one of the best.

1. STRATEGY & POSITIONING

•When you're on the dead side of the zone, rotate along the edge to avoid getting pinched. This means moving along the edge of the storm, where there are fewer players.

•Don't waste your Launch Pads, Shockwaves, or other mobility items. Instead, save them for crowded areas or the late game.

•You can assign building structures to your side mouse buttons so you can sprint continuously while building.

2. PIECE CONTROL

•Take control of structures like walls and floors around your opponent before shooting so they have fewer escape options.

•If your enemy tries to place a ramp to protect themselves, place a cone inside their box early.

•Slow down your edits slightly to make sure you select the correct building tile.

3. MOVEMENT & PEEKS

•Keep your crosshair at head height even while running in case an enemy appears. Many players ignore this, which can lead to them being eliminated unexpectedly.

•Time your gunshot to hit the exact moment an aggressive player breaks through your wall.

4. GUN HIERARCHY

•High-Damage Pump Shotgun: This is still one of the top choices in Build Mode. Get this gun, and your path to the top will be smoother.

•Scoped AR: Best for long-distance headshots. A decent weapon to add to your arsenal.

•Rapid-Fire: Best for following up after a shotgun hit.
